State   Form   39870( R/ S- 06) 
Reference Number 
SOC-003 
Subject 
Special Events – Planning and Coordination 
Special Instructions 
Replaces SOC-003 dated January 15, 2015 
Effective Date 
January 30, 2026 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for command, communications, and assignment of personnel to cover special 
events and the response to emergency situations. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
The Department shall provide personnel to handle vehicular traffic, crowd control, and related issues at 
assigned special events as needed. Under normal circumstances, the host agency will be the decision 
making entity for the event; however, personnel assigned to the special events must be aware that 
certain circumstances, such as imminent danger, may require discretionary decision making on their 
part. 
 
III. DEFINITIONS 
 
A. Event Commander – Either the Commander of the Special Operations Command (SOC) or a person 
designated by the SOC Commander or the Primary Staff to serve in this position. The event 
commander may, as needed, designate various duties and responsibilities listed within this procedure or 
the event action plan (EAP) to subordinate staff. 
 
B. Special Event – Any event designated as a special event by the SOC Commander or the Primary 
Staff. 
 
IV. PROCEDURE 
 
A. SPECIAL EVENTS 
 
1. The SOC Commander or a designee shall serve as the event commander during a designated special 
event.  The event commander of each special event shall: 
 
a. Designate personnel of adequate rank to serve as a command and general staff for the event. The size 
of the staff will be based upon the size and significance of the event; and 
 
b. Coordinate all pre-event planning and expectations for normal and emergency operations and 
command responsibility in accordance with NIMS (National incident Management System) procedures. 
 
2. Special Events Action Plan (EAP) 
 
a. For all large scale or annual special events an event action plan shall be prepared and shall contain: 
 
(i) A Non-Criminal Incident Report number assigned to the event (optional); 

(ii) A list of commanders and other Department personnel assigned to each detail; 
(iv) Explanation of all pertinent information for each detail; 
(v) A description of procedures for the arrest and processing of subjects taken into custody. The 
processing procedures for adult and juvenile offenders shall be explained separately; 
(vi) A listing of emergency and general use contact information (land line and cell phone numbers; as 
well as, pagers, email, etc.) for the host facility, all involved agencies and available services; 
(vii) A copy of the host facilities emergency operations plan; and 
(viii) A written understanding of the Department’s role, authority, and expectations under normal and 
emergency situations during the special event. If this information is not provided by the host 
facility it shall be noted in the CAD record for the event and in the Non-Criminal Incident Report 
if completed. 
 
3. Communications/Operations Center 
 
a. When the type or size of a special event requires a dedicated ISP communications/operations center, 
the event commander shall work with the Commander of Criminal Justice Data Division or a designee 
to identify and designate the proper type of communications center to be used.  The communications 
center may consist of: 
 
(i) A dispatch facility; 
(ii) A designated regional dispatch center with a regional or district duty officer, and an RDC 
dispatcher, etc.; or 
(iii) An on-site portable (command bus) or fixed communications center. 
 
b. An ISP communications center should: 
 
(i)  Be designed and equipped to handle emergency situations beyond normal routine event use. This 
may include access to the host facilities internal communications systems (land and cell phones, 
pagers, internet/email) as well as any available PA system, automated information boards, etc.; 
(ii)  Operate on a pre-determined dedicated designated “special ops” frequency and notify all 
participating agencies of available state wide mutual aid (SWMA) frequencies to be used in the 
event of an emergency; 
(iii)  When possible, obtain compatible communications equipment capable of monitoring and 
transmitting to other involved agencies; 
(iv)  Handle all Department special event radio, telephone, fax, and other communications; 
(v)  Follow all general communications procedures; 
(vi)  Maintain contact with the host facility operations center or staff personnel; 
(vii)  Make assignments to details as they arise during the special event; and 
(viii)  When directed by the event commander the communications center shall follow standard call out 
procedures at special events where specialty teams are not specifically assigned, but become 
needed in response to an incident. 
 
4. Facility Planning. 
 
a. The event commander and the event staff shall be familiar with the host facilities emergency action 
plan. If the host facility does not have an emergency action plan, then the event commander and event 
staff shall become familiar with: 
 
(i)  The identification of emergency shelters and access routes; 
(ii)  The identification of emergency ingress and egress routes and staging areas for responding 
public safety personnel, vehicles, and equipment; 
 
(iii)  Established evacuation routes and safe locations on the facility grounds (if available) for patrons 
of the event; 
(iv)  The location of nearby utility (electric, gas, water, etc.) infrastructure with emergency access 
routes to each location and emergency contact availability; and 
(v)  Available medical facilities, such as: 
 
(a) Pre-established patient triage centers with quick access in and out for emergency services 
personnel and ambulances, 
(b)  Landing zone for air ambulances; and 
(c)  A contingency plan for a temporary morgue facility in case of mass casualty deaths, and 
(d)  Adequate supply of or quick access to Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) such as protective 
gloves, HEPA masks, etc. 
 
5. Upon the completion of each special event, the team leader of each location may submit a summary 
type supplement report (to include the detail location) under the special events Non- Criminal Incident 
Report number when: 
 
a. Any event of any significance occurs at a location; 
b. An unusual high number of arrests are made (listing the number and type); 
c. The identification of any unusual problems at the specific location; and 
 
6. Any recommendations for improvements for subsequent details (to include but not limited to: the 
recommendation for the elimination of unnecessary details or locations; the merging or creation of new 
detail locations; or requests for additional or less staffing for a particular location). 
 
7. The event commander shall upon completion of the special event forward an after action report, 
through channels, to the SOC Commander providing a brief synopsis of the event to include any issues 
with pre-planning, operational or jurisdictional issues and recommendations (if necessary) for revisions 
to the special event action plan or the event its self . 
 
B. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, policies, 
and procedures.
