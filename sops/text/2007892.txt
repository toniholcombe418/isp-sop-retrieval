STANDARD OPERATING  PROCEDURE  
State   Form 39870 ( R/ S- 06 ) 
Reference Number 
TRA-011 
Subject 
Deactivation of Weapons Used in Special Training Programs 
Special Instructions 
Replaces TRA-011 dated August 18, 2023 
Effective Date 
October 20, 2025 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for firearms used in training programs other than firearms range practice and 
qualifications. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Department personnel engaged in training which utilizes a weapon for any purpose other than for live 
firearms training shall not use the weapon until it is modified so it cannot fire live ammunition and is 
marked to indicate to the casual observer the weapon is deactivated, or inoperable. 
 
III. DEFINITIONS 
 
A. MARKINGS – Grips of handguns and stocks of rifles and shotguns must be marked with the color 
indicating whether the training weapon has been deactivated, or made inoperable. 
 
1. GREEN – Weapon is completely inoperable (cannot be fired); 
 
2. BLUE – Weapon is capable of firing a non-lethal but potentially injurious projectile. (These 
weapons are operable, but deactivated, i.e. sim gun). 
 
3. RED PLASTIC – A weapon that is plastic or synthetic and not capable of firing. 
 
B. TRAINING WEAPON – A weapon designed or modified to be used in training exercises that  will 
not cause serious bodily injury, or death, when properly used. Any weapon which requires 
modification prior to use as a Training Weapon will be classified as: 
 
1. INOPERABLE – Altered to the point the firearm will not function. 
 
2. DEACTIVATED – Altered so the firearm is not capable of firing live ammunition. 
 
C. WEAPON – Any firearm (including, but not limited to: shotgun, rifle, or handgun) which is  
capable of firing a projectile, or projectiles, and has the potential to cause injury or death. 
 
IV. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Training Weapons shall be made inoperable by a Department armorer and indicated as inoperable 
by the following methods: 
 
1. Remove the hammer nose or firing pin; or 
 
2. Place a sleeve inside all cylinder or chamber openings to prevent loading of ammunition (live or

plastic); or 
 
3. Weld cylinder, slide, or action closed; and 
 
4. Mark grips and/or stocks green. 
 
B. Training weapons shall be deactivated by a Department armorer to allow the weapon to fire a non-
lethal projectile and indicated as deactivated by the following methods: 
 
1. Place a sleeve inside the cylinder or chamber to allow the loading of a casing from which only a 
non-injurious projectile may be fired; and 
 
C.  Grips and/or stocks must be marked with green or blue markings to be consistent with the   level of 
deactivation. 
 
D.  Training weapons will be utilized in, but not limited to: shooting decision programs,  FieldTactics 
& Techniques or any other force on force scenario-based training. 
 
E.  It will be the responsibility of the Safety Officer(s) involved in a training exercise to inspect and 
ensure all weapons used in the training exercise are either deactivated, or inoperable. The Safety 
Officer will ensure no live ammunition or weapons containing live ammunition are allowed into the 
training area. 
 
F.  Duty Weapons 
 
1. Duty weapons used during a training exercise will be rendered incapable of firing live ammunition 
or any type of training ammunition. 
 
2. Duty weapons will be marked to indicate to the casual observer the weapon has been modified to 
prevent the firing of any live ammunition or any type of training ammunition. 
 
G.  Blank ammunition will be fired only from training weapons specifically designed to fire blank 
ammunition.  
 
H.  When training weapons are used, a firearms instructor or designated safety officer must be present. 
 
I.  This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, policies, 
and procedures.
