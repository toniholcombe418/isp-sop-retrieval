STANDARD   OPERATING   PROCEDURE 
State   Form   39870( R/ S- 06) 
Reference Number 
ENF-018 
Subject 
Notifications: Death, Serious Injury or Illness (Non-Departmental) 
Special Instructions 
Replaces ENF-018 dated March 1, 2015 
Effective Date 
October 29, 2025 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for making notifications by Department members in cases involving death, serious 
injury or illness. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
When necessary, Department personnel shall notify the next of kin in all deaths, serious injury or illness 
cases investigated by the Department or when requested to make such notification by another agency. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. When necessary, Department personnel will personally make notifications to the next of kin in all 
incidents in which the Department has primary jurisdiction. 
 
1. Due to its sensitive nature, this duty must be approached with compassion and understanding. 
 
2. When requesting another district or agency to make a notification, it shall be requested that they confirm 
when the notification has been completed. 
 
B. When requested, personnel making a notification for another agency shall: 
 
1. Confirm the information provided is correct; 
2. Learn as much as possible regarding the circumstances of the incident; 
3. Provide necessary contact information to those persons notified; and 
4. Provide confirmation to the requesting agency after the notification is made. 
 
C. Department personnel may enlist the assistance of the Department Chaplain or district chaplains to 
accompany them on all death notifications, when available. 
 
D. When it is apparent that the physical or mental condition of the next of kin may complicate the 
notification, seek additional assistance from a relative, clergyman, physician, friend, or neighbor. 
 
E. Department personnel will notify the nearest military facility of the appropriate branch of service when the 
death, injury, or illness involves active military personnel. 
 
F. Department personnel will notify the nearest Embassy or Consulate of the death of a foreign national.  
Contact information can be located at U.S. Department of State – Bureau of Consular Affairs. 
 
G. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, policies, and 
procedures.
