I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for the safe collection, chain of custody, storage and disposal of explosive 
ordnances or related materials. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
To maintain safety and integrity of evidence storage areas, explosive ordnances or related 
materials will be stored separately and securely in an Explosive Storage Magazine.  Each item 
will maintain a chain of custody and be recorded on a property record receipt. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Scene processing and collection of evidence 
 
1. Explosives or Improvised Explosive Device (IED) materials that are not safe to collect and 
store as evidence, as determined by a Public Safety Bomb Technician (PSBT), shall be 
immediately destroyed by EOD personnel.  These materials shall be photographed prior to 
destruction, if safe to do so. 
 
2. All evidence collected that involves explosives or IED related components, will be collected 
by a PSBT, or under the supervision of a PSBT, if completed by a CSI investigator from the 
Indiana State Police (ISP) or another law enforcement agency.   
 
3. All evidence will be recovered/packaged/sealed, when feasible, to ISP’s evidence standards.  
If an item is too large, or not safe to package according to normal evidence procedures, the item 
will be tagged with the appropriate information.  If packaging sensitive explosives or explosive 
related items, the PSBT will follow guidelines from the FBI Hazardous Devices School and/or 
ATF.    
 
4. An ISP PR&R shall be used to document all evidence collected by PSBT’s.  If an evidence 
item can be stored through the normal ISP evidence submission process, that process should be 
utilized.  If the evidence cannot be stored through normal ISP evidence procedures for safety 
reasons, those items will be documented on a separate PR&R and stored in one of ISP’s 
explosive storage magazines.  Any changes in the chain of custody before the item is placed in a 
bunker will be documented on the PR&R.  It is permissible for individual evidence items from 
the same scene to be processed and submitted differently, depending on the nature of the item.

B. Magazine Access 
 
a. Only ISP PSBT’s shall have access to ISP’s explosive storage magazines.   
 
b. All ISP magazines will be secured with padlocks that adhere to ATF requirements.  All 
padlocks will be “keyed” the same.  
 
c. The bomb squad commander shall issue keys to ISP PSBT’s for storage magazines and 
maintain a log of current personal who are issued a key.   
 
d. If a PSBT’s issued key becomes lost or stolen, the PSBT will immediately notify the bomb 
squad commander.  The bomb squad commander will then begin the process of obtaining and 
implementing the replacement of all padlocks at ISP magazines.   
 
C. Evidence Storage in Explosive Storage Magazine 
 
1. Submission procedures: 
 
a. All evidentiary items that will be stored in a magazine will be documented on a Property 
Record Receipt (PR&R).   
 
b. A physical copy of the PR&R will be placed with the evidence item when it is stored in a 
bunker.   
 
c. A digital copy of the same PR&R will be attached to the RMS incident report documenting the 
evidence’s status, chain of custody, and storage location.   
 
2. Evidence Removal and/or Changes in Chain of Custody 
 
a. Any change in the chain of custody and/or removal of evidence from the magazine for testing, 
analysis, comparison, storage location change, destruction/disposal, and/or for court related  
activities, shall be documented on the physical copy of the PR&R stored with the evidence item.  
 
b. All changes in evidence status described in section a. above, shall be documented in a 
supplemental RMS incident report.   
 
c. A supplemental digital copy of the PR&R shall also be attached to the supplemental report, 
documenting the changes.     
 
d. Upon placing new EOD evidence into a Explosive Storage Magazine, the officer shall notify 
the bomb squad commander that new evidence was placed into the Magazine. 
 
3. EOD Disposal 
 
a. Any EOD evidence that are identified which can be purged/disposed/destroyed, shall be 
documented, and preparations made for disposal.   
3 
 
b. After disposal, the PSBT responsible for the evidence (case officer) shall complete a 
supplemental RMS report documenting the action.   
 
D. Inventory/Audit 
 
a. An ISP PSBT will be assigned by the bomb squad commander to each magazine for inventory 
and audit documentation.  An evidence audit/purge inspection shall occur at least once a year, 
concurrently with one of the monthly inventories.   
 
b. The ISP PSBT responsible for the magazine shall be present, along with at least one other ISP 
PSBT.   
 
c. The annual inventory/audit shall be documented by the PSBT assigned to the magazine by: 
 
1. Pulling an RMS incident report to document all evidentiary items currently in the magazine 
and their status; and 
 
2. Comparing all items to inventory/audit list maintained for each magazine. 
 
E. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies, and procedures.
