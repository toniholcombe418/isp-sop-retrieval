I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for Department personnel assigned to the Commercial Vehicle Enforcement 
Division (CVED) as required by IC 10-11-3. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Department personnel assigned to CVED shall enforce all provisions of the Indiana Code and all 
federal rules and regulations pertaining to the operation of commercial motor vehicles; in addition, 
to obtaining and maintaining all required federal certifications. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Personnel 
 
1. Motor Carrier Inspectors (MCI) (Civilian) are charged with the duties of supervising and 
operating permanent or portable weight stations and inspection facilities. Additionally, MCIs have 
the authority to enforce the specific statutes as described in IC 10-11-2-26. 
 
2. Commercial Vehicle Enforcement Officers (CVEO) (Troopers) are charged with specific duties 
of technical enforcement and investigations relevant to commercial motor vehicle safety, state 
school bus inspections and hazardous materials compliance. 
 
3. The CVED Commander shall be responsible for the administration of the motor carrier 
enforcement function of the Department with the assistance of designated police, MCIs, and 
professional staff. 
 
B. Duties and Responsibilities 
 
1. MCIs shall be required to successfully complete training with the National Training Center; and 
when required, in Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations, Hazardous Materials Regulations and 
Safety Audits. 
 
a. MCI’s duties shall include but are not limited to: 
 
(i) Perform commercial vehicle inspections; 
 
(ii) Conducting selected training programs for enforcement personnel; 
 
(iii) Size and weight enforcement; 
 
(iv) assist with the Department’s school bus inspection program;

(v) Assisting in commercial motor vehicle post-crash inspection and investigations; 
 
(vi) Assisting the Department of Revenue with dyed fuel enforcement; 
 
(vii) Commercial motor vehicle post-crash inspections, as outlined in CVE-007; 
 
(viii) All other violations as described in IC 10-11-2-26; and 
 
(ix) All other duties as required by the CVED Commander. 
 
2. CVEO 
 
a. IC 10-11-3 requires the section to consist of a Chief Enforcement Officer and (at least) 19 
CVEO’s. 
 
b. CVEO’s shall be required to successfully complete training with the National Training Center; 
and when required, in Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations, Hazardous Materials Regulations 
and compliance reviews. 
 
c. CVEO’s duties shall include but are not limited to: 
 
(i) Perform commercial vehicle traffic enforcement and inspections; 
 
(ii) Conducting selected training programs for enforcement personnel; 
 
(iii) Compliance reviews of intrastate and interstate carriers; 
 
(iv) Coordinate or assist with the Department’s School Bus Inspection program; 
 
(v) Assisting in Commercial motor vehicle post-crash inspection and investigations; 
 
(vi) Assisting the Department of Revenue with dyed fuel enforcement; 
 
(vii) Size and weight enforcement; 
 
(viii) Fulfilling the normal duties as required of an Indiana State Police Trooper; and 
 
(iv) All other duties as required by the CVED Commander. 
 
C. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies and procedures.
