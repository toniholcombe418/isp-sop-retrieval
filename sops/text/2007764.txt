State   Form   39870( R/ S- 06) 
Reference Number 
ENF-038 
Subject 
Plain Patrol Commission Assignment 
Special Instructions 
Replaces ENF-038 dated March 1, 2015 
Effective Date 
October 29, 2025 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for the assignment and administration of the Department’s plain patrol 
commission program. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Area commanders shall be responsible for assigning and administering the use of the plain patrol 
commissions within their respective areas of operation. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Plain patrol commission(s) shall be assigned at the discretion of the area commander. 
 
B. District commanders may recommend a trooper to receive a plain patrol commission to the area 
commander. 
 
C. Area commanders shall reassign plain patrol commissions when an assigned trooper is promoted, 
transferred, or reassigned. This does not prohibit the reassignment of the commission for any other 
cause the area commander may deem appropriate. 
 
D. Issuing guide. 
 
1. The following should be considered when assessing the assignment of plain patrol commissions: 
 
a. Previous enforcement activities for the trooper being considered; 
b. Individual troopers should not routinely be issued consecutive plain patrol commissions; 
however, when appropriate circumstances warrant such issuance a commander is not prohibited 
from doing so; and 
c. Probationary Troopers should not be issued plain patrol commissions without sufficient 
justification. 
 
E. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies, and procedures.
