STANDARD   OPERATING   PROCEDURE 
State   Form   39870 ( R/ S- 06) 
Reference Number 
CVE-002 
Subject 
Motor Carrier Safety Assistance Program (MCSAP) 
Special Instructions 
Replaces CVE-003 dated March 1, 2015 
Effective Date 
August 13, 2024 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for implementing the Motor Carrier Safety Assistance Program (MCSAP) as 
described in IC 8-2.1-24-18, which adopted 49 CFR Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations and 
the Hazardous Materials Regulations. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Only Department personnel, trained to enforce the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations 
(FMCSR) and/or Hazardous Material Regulations (HMR), shall be responsible to enforce these 
regulations, through authorized motor carrier safety inspections following the programs federal 
guidelines. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. The MCSAP Comprehensive Policy and the CVED Policy Manual outlines the procedures for 
this program. 
 
B. MCSAP is administered by the Commercial Vehicle Enforcement Division (CVED). Billing 
procedures to the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) for grant reimbursement 
shall be accomplished in the Fiscal Section. 
 
C. The CVED shall normally be responsible for training of all personnel involved in MCSAP 
related activities. Such training shall be coordinated with the Training Division. 
 
D. All equipment required to complete MCSAP activities shall be accounted for by the CVED and 
coordinated with the Field Support Services (FSS). 
 
E. MCSAP data functions shall be administered by the CVED; in conjunction with the Indiana 
Office of Technology and the Communications and Information System Division. 
 
F. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies and procedures.
