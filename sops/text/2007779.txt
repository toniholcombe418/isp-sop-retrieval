STANDARD OPERATING  PROCEDURE  
State   Form 39870 ( R/ S- 06 ) 
Reference Number 
LOG-005 
Subject 
Basic Equipment for Department Commissions 
Special Instructions 
Replaces FSS-005 dated January 15, 2015 
Effective Date 
March 10, 2026 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for the basic equipment to be carried in Department commissions. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Department police employees shall routinely carry in their assigned commission the equipment outlined in this 
procedure.  An officer’s primary duty assignment (administrative or patrol) may dictate some of the required 
equipment to be issued and/or carried in the commission. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. All Department commissions shall routinely carry the following equipment: 
 
1. Tactical Med Kit 5. Crash Investigation equipment and report forms* 
2. Fire Extinguisher 6. Extra Handcuffs 
3. Fuses 7. ANSI Reflective Vest 
4. Ticket/Warning Books*  
 
*These items may be replaced by Department Technology (issued computer and related programs). 
 
B. Department commissions assigned to road patrol duties shall also routinely carry the following additional 
equipment: 
 
1. Tape measure 4. Rechargeable flashlight 7. PBT 
2. Camera: Digital or Dept. phone w/ Capture App. 5. Laptop computer 8. Rifle with duty rounds 
3. Tire deflation devices 6. Inclement Weather Clothing 9. AED 
10. Rifle Plate Carrier    
 
C. Issued body armor, if not worn by police employees, shall be carried in their assigned commission or the 
commission the employee is using and/or shall be kept near the officer, so it is quickly accessible. 
 
D. Officers assigned to a Department specialty team (e.g., SCUBA, SWAT, MFF, etc.) shall carry in their 
commissions the issued equipment as required by other Department directives and/or SOPs, unless otherwise 
exempted. 
 
E. This policy should not restrict officers from routinely carrying other equipment specifically authorized or 
approved. 
 
F. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, policies and 
procedures.
