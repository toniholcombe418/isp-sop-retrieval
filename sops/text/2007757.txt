I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines as to the functions of the Department’s patrol component. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
The Department’s patrol component shall provide the most efficient delivery of services possible and 
take appropriate enforcement action for violations of the law witnessed or reported, in order to gain 
compliance with criminal and traffic laws. Officer’s actions should demonstrate a professional attitude 
and serve to improve the relationship between the public and the Department. 
 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Functions of the patrol component: 
 
1. Patrol their assigned area to include county, state, and federal roadways to prevent, detect, and 
apprehend law violators. 
 
a. Troopers shall be proactive in carrying out their patrol duties; 
 
b. All enforcement actions shall be accomplished in a firm, fair, impartial, and courteous manner using 
one of the following three methods, 
 
(i) Written warning, 
(ii) Citation, and/or 
(iii) Physical arrest. 
 
2. Investigate traffic crashes and issue citations or warnings, as required; 
 
3. Aid disabled or lost motorists; 
 
4. Attempt to locate drivers and/or other persons to deliver emergency information; 
 
5. Direct and control traffic at crash sites or special events (e.g. race details, state fair, etc.); 
 
6. Render first aid to injured persons until the arrival of trained or certified medical help; 
 
7. Complete and submit Trooper Activities section (Daily Activity and Duty Hours report) in SHIELD  
as outlined in ENF-044 to accurately account for those activities in which the officer participated and 
the time spent on each; 

2  
 
8. Transport emergency medical supplies, blood, and human organs; 
 
9. Conduct inspections of commercial motor vehicles; 
 
10. Assist the Federal Aviation Administration in the investigation of aircraft crashes; 
 
11. Conduct criminal investigations; 
 
12. Testify in court; 
 
13. Serve as a member of the Department’s specialty teams, when qualified and selected as outlined in 
SOC-004; 
 
14. Present information on traffic safety, dangerous drugs and other relevant topics of public safety.  
15. Represent the Indiana State Police at special events or public meetings and provide information to 
the news media 
 
16. May be required to assume the district duty officer’s position during absence of the district 
commander and other supervisors. 
 
17. Conduct duties as required and as directed by supervisors or command personnel. 
 
B. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, policies, 
and procedures.
