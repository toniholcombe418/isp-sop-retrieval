I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for investigating and reporting vehicle crashes and provides deadlines for 
submitting reports to the applicable district and crash repository. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Department personnel investigating motor vehicle crashes resulting in fatality, injury, or property 
damage (meeting state requirements) shall document the crash using the approved crash reporting 
method. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Department personnel when aware of, and applicable, shall respond to the scene of a motor 
vehicle crash to assist with or investigate and complete a crash report whenever there is: 
 
1. Injury or death of a person; or 
2. Total property damage (e.g. vehicles, cargo, and other property) of $2,500.00 or more; and 
3. The crash occurred on a trafficway or private property that is open to the public. 
 
B. Department personnel shall also investigate and report, if applicable, any crash involving: 
 
1. Any state police vehicle; 
2. If available, all other state owned vehicles or property; 
3. Any apparent impairment of a participant due to alcohol or drugs; or 
4. Any time (regardless of the amount of property damage) there is: 
 
(i)  A violation of law, which is the primary or a contributing cause of the crash; 
(ii)  A disagreement or conflict between the parties involved; 
(iii) One of the participants illegally leaves the scene of the crash; or 
(iv)  The crash involves hazardous materials; and; 
 
5. Whenever the vehicle or vehicles have been moved from the scene and the crash meets the criteria 
set forth in section III. A. 1-3. 
 
C. Officers at the scene shall: 
 
1. Evaluate the scene for hazards and initiate necessary protective measures to include staying a safe 
distance from the scene if the proper protective equipment is not available; 
 
2. Ensure the applicable regional dispatch center and appropriate command personnel are kept aware 
of the scene status; 

2  
 
3. Provide immediate care for the injured and request additional assistance as needed; 
 
4. Ensure the property from a vehicle is secured if the owner is unable to care for it; if necessary, 
secure and properly inventory valuable items to be released to the owner later; 
 
5. Obtain all information and conduct an appropriate investigation, including photographs and 
measurements, to complete the report; and 
 
6. The investigating officer shall provide each driver with a driver’s exchange of information form 
or similar document and should advise each driver to immediately notify their insurance company of 
their involvement in a crash. 
 
7. It is the investigating officer’s responsibility to ensure that crash investigation photographs are 
taken at all crash scenes, if a crash report is completed. 
 
D. Activation of Area Crash Investigation & Reconstruction Teams: 
 
1. Members of the Area Crash Investigation & Reconstruction Team (CIRT) team may be requested 
by other agencies, Department personnel at the scene, or by district command when crashes 
involves: 
 
a. Serious bodily injury or death; 
b. Other special circumstances, which may include: 
 
(i)  Crashes involving emergency vehicles (police, fire, or ambulance), 
(ii) Government owned vehicles, or 
(iii)  Commercial Motor Vehicles. 
 
c. CIRT may also be called to assist with the investigation of large-scale incidents which require 
forensic mapping of the scenes should the need arise. 
 
2. District personnel will notify the CIRT team leader of the available crash details and provide 
contact information for the crash scene officer-in-charge (OIC). 
 
3. The CIRT team leader will immediately contact the crash scene OIC and obtain the specific facts 
of the preliminary crash investigation and determine the immediate need for a CIRT member to 
respond to the scene. Once the determination is made, the CIRT leader will notify a CIRT 
member(s) for a response in addition to updating the district personnel as to who is responding and 
an approximate estimated time of arrival (ETA). 
 
E. The Commercial Vehicle Enforcement Division (CVED) shall be contacted if the crash involves: 
 
1. A load of hazardous materials (not including the loss of fuel from a vehicle fuel tank); 
 
2. A school bus or passenger motor coach (in the business of transporting passengers); and 
 
3. Anytime a commercial motor vehicle (CMV) is involved in a personal injury or fatal crash or any

crash where the operation of the CMV or its equipment comes into question. CVED involvement 
may require additional inspection or investigation by CVED personnel. Refer to CVE-007 regarding 
specific criteria and action required. 
 
F. Crashes involving fatal injuries. 
 
1. Fatal vehicle crashes investigated by the Department shall be reported to the Criminal Justice 
Institute, using the Fatal Accident Reporting System (FARs) report, as soon as the information is 
available. 
 
2. A supplemental crash report shall be submitted to ARIES whenever toxicology reports are 
received  regarding a fatal crash. 
 
3. All ARIES reports involving fatalities should be sent to the Crash Reconstruction Coordinator and 
the respective Area Crash Reconstruction Team Leader, after direct supervisor review, for a final 
review before being uploaded into the ARIES Portal. 
 
4. Any ARIES report completed that involves a police pursuit that results in serious bodily injury or 
death shall be submitted to the Area Crash Reconstruction Team Leader for final review prior to 
submission into the ARIES portal. 
 
G. Chemical tests for intoxicants from drivers involved in a fatal vehicle crash (IC 9-13-2- 22 and IC 
9-27-5-4) (breath or blood). 
 
1. To the maximum extent practicable, and in a manner consistent with legal rights and Implied 
Consent, chemical test for intoxicants (breath or blood) shall be given to drivers including surviving 
drivers involved in a fatal vehicle crash and pedestrians 15 years of age or older, if deceased within 
four (4) hours of the vehicle crash. 
 
2. Fluid blood samples obtained from a driver(s) involved in a fatal vehicle crash (including 
surviving drivers and pedestrians 15 years of age or older) and, if of evidential value in pending 
criminal prosecution, shall be forwarded to the appropriate laboratory for analysis. Samples taken 
from surviving drivers shall be taken only by properly trained medical personnel. 
 
H. Notice to Owner of the Site of an Accident: 
 
1. The investigating officer shall notify the owner of real property, if a crash results in: 
 
a. Damage to the owner’s real property, crops, building, structure, or fixture attached to the owner’s 
real property; or 
b. Debris from a crash is left on the owner’s real property that could result in damage to farm 
equipment or other vehicles or property. 
 
2.  The investigating officer shall enter on the Computer Aided Dispatch (CAD) that notification is 
required and when completed.  The CAD shall not be closed until notification is completed. 
 
3.  If the officer cannot make notification prior to the conclusion of their shift, the CAD shall remain 
open and an officer on the next shift shall be assigned to make notification.

I. Completing and Submitting crash reports: Department personnel shall: 
 
1. Utilize the ARIES electronic crash reporting system to complete and submit reports; 
 
2. Complete all crash reports following the guidelines outlined in the ARIES Crash Report manual, 
ISP crash investigation training and applicable state laws; 
 
3. All crash narratives shall follow the approved format and include: 
 
a. A description of the involved vehicle(s) behavior before, during and after the crash; 
b. Statements from each driver(s) involved.  Written statements by each driver may be included as 
an attachment; 
c. Statements from each Witness or other participant (if applicable). Written statements by each 
participant shall be included as an attachment, and 
d. The investigating officer’s explanation as to the determination of the causation factor(s). 
 
4. Submit reports electronically through ARIES by the third day following the crash; and 
 
5. The report shall be transmitted through ARIES as soon as approved. 
 
6. When a crash reconstruction is completed (either full reconstruction or on-scene assistance by 
CIRT team members) in addition to the ARIES crash report, a criminal incident titled Crash 
Reconstruction shall be initiated. The additional offense of Death Investigation – Traffic may be 
added as a secondary title to the incident if it is a fatal crash. 
 
J. In situations involving ISP pursuits, all ARIES crash reports shall be reviewed by the respective 
Area Crash Reconstruction Team Leader, after the district review, prior to submission into the 
ARIES portal.  This includes those crashes involving property damage only. 
 
K. Vehicle crashes involving deer. 
 
1. All deer injured or killed by a collision with a motor vehicle shall be disposed of in the following 
manner: 
 
a. Any injured deer shall be destroyed in accordance with ENF-011. 
 
b. Any deer killed or destroyed may be released to the owner or driver of the damaged vehicle or to 
anyone else at the discretion of the investigating officer. Priority shall be given to the owner or 
driver of the vehicle involved; 
 
c. If two or more deer are involved, the owner or driver shall be eligible for only one deer.  Any or 
all other deer may be released to anyone other than the driver of the vehicle involved; 
 
d. A permit (State Form #38460) from the Department of Natural Resources shall be issued by the 
investigating officer to all persons granted possession of any or all deer killed or destroyed. The 
permit allows possession of the deer for a period not to exceed 60 days; and 
 
e. If no one at the scene wants the deer, the district duty officer shall request the removal of any or 
5  
all deer by the appropriate highway department or other means. 
 
f. Non-residents are also eligible to receive a deer. 
 
L. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies and procedures.
