STANDARD   OPERATING   PROCEDURE 
State   Form   39870 ( R/ S- 06) 
Reference Number 
ENF-039 
Subject 
Staff, District, Squad Meetings  
Special Instructions 
Replaces ENF-039 dated March 1, 2015 
Effective Date 
February 24, 2026 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for conducting periodic meetings at the staff, district and squad levels. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
All applicable commanders shall conduct training updates and informational meetings with 
personnel as needed or as outlined in this procedure. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Commanders at all levels are encouraged to conduct staff meetings, as needed, to facilitate the 
dissemination of information and discuss pertinent issues. 
 
B. District commanders should conduct a district-wide meeting at least two (2) times annually. 
 
1. Such meetings shall be attended by all enforcement personnel assigned to that district. 
Professional staff at the district shall attend when matters pertaining to them are discussed. 
 
2. District Commanders shall document who is in attendance at the meeting. 
 
3. An agenda outlining the topics of discussion shall be developed and then filed at the district. 
 
a. Though the specific agenda will vary for each meeting, certain topic areas should be addressed  at 
each meeting. 
 
(i) Discussion of legal updates; 
(ii) Discussion of problems relating to traffic and criminal matters. 
(iii) Review progress toward Department and district goals and objectives. 
(iv) Training updates to supplement formal training sessions. 
 
C. Squad meetings should be conducted on a quarterly basis at an appropriate time as to not disrupt 
operational needs and cover necessary information. 
 
D. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies and procedures.
