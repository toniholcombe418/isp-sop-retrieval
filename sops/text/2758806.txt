I. PURPOSE 
 
Establishes the Department policy for a spot bonus that is awarded to employees based on 
performance or achievements that deserve special recognition. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
The Spot Bonus program is designed to recognize exceptional performance by employees 
through a monetary spot bonus award. Individual and/or team/group contributions may also be 
recognized. Under this program, recipients may receive no more than two spot bonus awards 
within one fiscal year, not to exceed more than $1,000.00 for any single award or exceed 
$2,000.00 cumulatively. 
 
III. DEFINITIONS 
   
A. APPROVING AUTHORITY - The Superintendent, or designee, that is responsible for final 
approval of the spot bonus. 
 
B. NOMINATOR – any employee of the Department may nominate another employee for a spot 
bonus that meets the criteria below.  A nominator may be the supervisor of the nominee, or 
another employee.  Employees may not nominate themselves for a spot bonus. 
 
C. NOMINEE – any Department employee (in good standing) nominated to receive a spot bonus 
as outlined in this policy. 
 
D. SPOT BONUS – a monetary one-time award of a specified dollar amount (not added to base 
salary) granted to an eligible employee that meets the criteria below. A spot bonus is subject to 
income tax withholdings and any regular percentage benefits withholdings. 
 
IV. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA 
 
A. To qualify for a spot bonus as outlined in this policy, the nominee must: 
 
1. Not have been the recipient of a work improvement plan, disciplinary action, or letter of 
counseling in the six (6) months prior to nomination; 
2. Have been successful in meeting all performance expectations and competencies; and 
3. Have met at least one of the following criteria to include, but not limited to: 
 
a. Demonstrate exceptional performance, completion of a project ahead of schedule with results 
that exceeded expectations, creation of a solution to a problem, or provided exceptional customer

service (internal and/or external); 
b. Assumed and successfully performed additional workload for a significant period; or 
c. Accomplished a task or project that falls outside or beyond the scope of the employee’s 
regular day-to-day tasks and assignments. 
 
V. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Spot bonus nomination, processing, and approval must follow these procedures: 
 
1. The nominator shall prepare and submit the Spot Bonus Nomination Form to the nominee’s 
supervisor. 
 
2. The nominee's supervisor shall verify compliance with eligibility criteria and determining 
whether a reward is appropriate.  
 
3. If appropriate, the nominee's supervisor shall endorse the Spot Bonus Nomination Form and 
forward it, if applicable, to their next in command for endorsement up through their respective 
Major or Director.  
 
4. Once the chain of command of the employee has completed endorsement, the Spot Bonus 
Nomination Form shall be forwarded to the Human Resources Director for processing. 
 
5. The Human Resources Director is responsible to:  
 
a. Ensure the nomination form is complete; 
b. Submit the nomination to the Superintendent, or designee, for review; 
c. Submit the form to the Fiscal Director, if approved by the Superintendent; and 
d. Notify the employee’s respective commander the award has been approved or denied. 
 
6. The Fiscal Director is responsible to: 
 
a. Complete payment processing when a bonus has been awarded, tracking the annual bonus 
compensation total, and ensuring compliance with FMC 2021-1; 
b. Notify the nominee's supervisor of the bonus approval and the date of deposit; 
c. Return the completed Spot Bonus Nomination Form to the Human Resources Director for 
retention in the employee’s personnel file; 
d. Track spot bonus award amounts of all ISP personnel on a Fiscal Year basis. 
 
B. Notification Process 
 
The employee’s supervisor notifies the employee of the spot bonus, after it has been approved at 
all levels and processed through payroll.
