Classified Materials – Assistance (Security DCS) 
Special Instructions 
Replaces ENF-058 dated January 1, 2012 
Effective Date 
March 1, 2015 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for Department assistance at incidents involving the Defense Courier 
Service (DCS). 
 
II. POLICY 
 
The Department shall assist the Defense Courier Service in the preservation and 
protection of shipments of classified materials transported upon the highways when such 
shipments have been interrupted by an unplanned event or attack. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Defense Courier Service (DCS) shipments are classified materials, thus requiring 
special handling. The materials do not bear classified markings and do not contain 
radioactive materials. 
 
B. DCS vehicles will be Government sedans with a Government “GSA” plate. 
 
C. A DCS courier will always have in their possession a DCS identification (ID) card. 
The ID card will be of United States Government issue, bearing a photograph and a 
physical description of the courier. The couriers will not be armed during transport of 
classified shipments. 
 
D. When the Department receives a request for assistance from a courier service 
involving a shipment of classified materials the applicable district shall immediately 
dispatch the nearest available officer to the scene. If the courier is incapacitated, there 
will be a placard somewhere in the cab of the vehicle with proper instructions. The 
responding officer shall: 
 
1. Secure the scene, preventing access by unauthorized persons; and 
 
2. Contact the appropriate district outlining the reason for the delay and other pertinent 
information needed for notification of the necessary federal agency; 
 
E. The district shall notify the nearest military installation or FBI office and Operations 
and forward all pertinent information relative to the delay of the classified materials 
shipment. 

Emergency Defense Courier Service numbers:

2. Second call (24 hours a day seven days a week):

b. After hours: 618-219-6002 
 
F. In situations where unauthorized individuals are exposed to classified materials 
Department enforcement personnel may request the individual allow the defense courier 
time to debrief them and request they sign-off on an Inadvertent Discloser Agreement. 
 
G. DCS couriers are subject to the same enforcement procedures as defined in ENF-022. 
 
H. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, 
rules, policies, and procedures.
