STANDARD OPERATING  PROCEDURE  
State   Form 39870 ( R/ S- 06 ) 
Reference Number 
INV-010 
Subject 
Procedure When Public Officials Become Targets of Investigations 
Special Instructions 
Replaces INV-003 dated April 4, 2024 
Effective Date 
October 24, 2025 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establishes guidelines for reporting instances when elected or appointed public officials (to include all 
law enforcement officers) become targets or subjects of Department investigations. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Any Department employee, who encounters an elected or appointed public official as a target or 
subject of an investigation shall make notification as explained in Sections B below, as soon as 
possible. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. The following information shall be reported regarding the public official: 
 
1. Name; 
 
2. Place of employment (agency, office, location); 
 
3. Position and title; 
 
4. Specific allegation(s), complaint(s), and charge(s); and 
 
5. How to contact investigating officer(s). 
 
B.  When an elected or appointed public official becomes the target of an investigation, or reaches the 
arrest phase, all information shall be collected and submitted through the electronic reporting system 
to the Investigations Bureau Commander or designee. (e.g. The public official has been arrested for a 
misdemeanor or felony, in a raid, or has been witnessed to have participated in a criminal act or is the 
subject of a criminal investigation).  
 
C. The Investigations Bureau Commander shall notify and update the Superintendent, or designee, 
when an elected or appointed public official becomes the target of an investigation.  
 
D. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies, and procedures.
