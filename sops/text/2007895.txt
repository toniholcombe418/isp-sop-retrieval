STANDARD   OPERATING   PROCEDURE 
State   Form   39870( R/ S- 06) 
Reference Number 
PIO-004 
Subject 
Crime Prevention and Community Relations 
Special Instructions 
Replaces PIO-004 dated March 1, 2015 
Effective Date 
October 17, 2023 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for the creation, implementation, coordination, evaluation, and 
maintenance of the Department’s crime prevention and community relations programs. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Department employees, as designated in this procedure, shall process and maintain 
Department approved crime prevention and community relations programs to meet 
community needs, concerns, and problems. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. The Chief PIO shall, upon request of the Superintendent, submit a report on the 
Department’s community relations programs, to the Office of the Superintendent, for 
review and approval. 
 
B. The Public Information Office shall: 
 
1. Be responsible for the creation, implementation, maintenance, coordination, and 
evaluation of the Department’s crime prevention, traffic safety, and community relations 
programs; 
 
2. Gather and evaluate information and develop programs relative to community needs, 
concerns, and problems, and evaluate the Department’s crime prevention, traffic safety, 
and community relations programs, as needed; and 
 
3. Create or utilize programs for presentation by agency personnel that address 
specific types of crimes in geographic areas. 
 
C. PIO shall research the needs and concerns of the communities of their respective 
assigned regions and create, implement, and maintain programs within their respective 
assigned districts.  They shall: 
 
1. Coordinate programs for community groups and local agencies, addressing perceptions 
and misperceptions of crime; 
 
2. Establish liaison(s) with foreign language specialists, business communities, citizens 
groups, and statewide organizations; 

2  
 
3. Facilitate programs in areas targeted for crime prevention and traffic safety activities 
including, but not limited to: 
 
a. Child identification programs; 
 
b. Drug-related programs for schools and community groups; and 
 
c. Identification of local types of crimes. 
 
d. Crash prevention and general traffic safety; 
 
e. New laws related to traffic safety. 
 
D. Each District Commander shall: 
 
1. Solicit information from the communities in their service area relative to practices and 
attitudes that may contribute to community tension and grievances; and 
 
2. Monitor crime prevention, traffic safety, and community relations programs and ensure 
promotion of these programs by all district personnel. 
 
E. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, 
rules, policies, and procedures.
