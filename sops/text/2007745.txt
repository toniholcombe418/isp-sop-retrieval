STANDARD   OPERATING   PROCEDURE 
State   Form   39870( R/ S- 06) 
Reference Number 
ENF-026 
Subject 
Interstate Highway Patrol 
Special Instructions 
Replaces ENF-008 dated January 1, 2012 
Effective Date 
March 1, 2015 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for patrolling the interstate highway system, throughout Indiana. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
The Department shall provide effective traffic/criminal enforcement and the most efficient police 
services possible to citizens using the interstate highway system. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. The Department shall maintain a presence on the interstate highway system by providing 
enforcement patrols and public service as required. 
 
B. District command personnel shall plan and schedule patrols for all interstate highways 
throughout their district with the exception of the Indiana Toll Road (I-80/90). Districts (other 
than the Toll Road) are not required to provide or schedule regular patrols of the Indiana Toll 
Road under normal conditions. 
 
C. Disabled or abandoned vehicles, parked along the interstate, shall be removed in conjunction 
with IC 9-13-2, IC 9-21-16-3, and IC 9-22-1, and in compliance with SOPs ENF-031 and ENF- 
008, and with all appropriate reports submitted as required. 
 
D. Pedestrians, non-motorized traffic, and other unauthorized vehicles shall be removed from 
interstate highways. Enforcement action should be taken in aggravated cases. 
 
E. The “No Parking on the Interstate” statute shall be enforced to include exit (on/off) ramps. 
 
F. At times when the Department is occupied with a civil disorder or disaster, a request may be 
originated by the Assistant Chief of Staff Operations to the Indiana Department of 
Transportation for a service patrol to monitor that portion of the interstate highway system not 
being patrolled by ISP personnel. 
 
G. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies, and procedures.
