I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for a plan of action to be taken by police employee’s arriving on the 
scene of a major crime. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Available police employees shall assist at the scene of major crimes. When 
investigations personnel are unavailable, uniformed officers shall proceed with the 
investigation. 
 
III. DEFINTIONS 
 
A. MAJOR CRIME SCENE – instances that involve death, attempted murder, child 
molestation, rape, or other sexual offenses against minors.   
 
    IV. PROCEDURE 
 
A. The first police employees arriving at the scene of a major crime shall: 
 
1. Proceed with caution in the event the suspects are in the immediate area. Then after 
securing the scene remain at the scene to assist with or conduct the investigation; 
2. Determine if medical assistance is required by any victims; 
3. Protect the scene from intrusion by unauthorized persons; 
4. Obtain descriptions of any possible suspects and vehicles; 
5. Obtain names, addresses, and telephone numbers of witnesses; 
6. Not remove any evidence, taking notes indicating the location and brief description of 
articles present;;  
7. As soon as practical, notify the District Investigative Commander (DIC) to request a 
detective be assigned to investigate;  
8. Upon the arrival of investigative personnel disseminate any information  obtained; 
and 
9. If investigations personnel are unavailable, uniformed officers shall proceed with the 
investigation. 
 
B. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, 
rules, policies, and procedures.
