Breath Test for Intoxication Operators 
Special Instructions 
Replaces TRA-006 dated July 9, 2018 
Effective Date 
August 8, 2023 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for certification and recertification of Department breath test operators 
pursuant to I.C. 9-30-6.  
 
II. POLICY 
 
Pursuant to specific rules adopted by the State Department of Toxicology only certified operators 
and equipment shall be used to administer breath tests for intoxicants. 
 
Recruit academy graduates shall attend a Breath Test for Intoxication School as soon as practical 
after graduation. Capitol Police Section (CPS) officers may attend a Breath Test for Intoxication 
School upon completion of their initial probationary period. 
 
Certified breath test operators may request exemption from recertifying by showing cause. 
Personnel who have been granted an exemption may not attend a Breath Test for Intoxication 
School to regain certification without the approval of the Commander of the Training Division. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Certification Process. 
 
1. The Training Division shall maintain a file of eligible candidates and notify their respective 
Commanders when a candidate will attend an available Breath Test for Intoxication School. 
 
2. Based upon the eligibility list and the needs of the Department, the Commander of the 
Training Division shall assign eligible police employees to attend a Breath Test for Intoxication 
School. 
 
3. Only those police employees successfully completing the Breath Test School for Intoxication 
shall be certified as operators. 
 
B. Recertification Process. 
 
1. Once certified, an operator is authorized to conduct breath tests for two (2) years before 
recertification is required. Certifications expire on the last day of the month two (2) years from 
the month of certification. 
 
2. The Training Division shall maintain a file of certified operators and assign them to recertify 
during the month prior to their certification’s expiration in accordance with guidelines and 
procedures established by the by the Indiana State Department of Toxicology.

3. Certified operators shall recertify only when directed to do so by the Training Division. 
 
C. Failure to pass the requirements of the Breath Testing for Intoxication School or 
recertification training shall be appropriately documented in the employee’s performance 
evaluations. 
 
D. Requests for Exemptions from Recertification. 
 
1. Police employees requesting exemption from recertification shall submit a memorandum, 
through channels, to the Commander of the Training Division. 
 
a. The memorandum shall include the employee’s present duty assignment and the reason 
exemption has been requested. The requirements for exemption are a minimum of 3 years  
removed from  road patrol duties; other special circumstances may be considered on a case-by-
case basis. 
 
b. The Commander of the Training Division shall review and approve or deny the request based 
upon the employee’s commander’s recommendation. 
 
c. If the exemption is granted, the Commander of the Training Division shall notify the employee 
of the status of the request. 
 
2. Only police personnel who have requested an exemption in writing and have been approved 
by the Commander of the Training Division will be excused from the obligation of retaining 
certification. 
 
3. Employees granted the exemption shall not violate the provisions of IC 9-30-6. 
E. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant department regulations, rules, 
policies, and procedures.
