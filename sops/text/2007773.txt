I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for providing protection and transportation for the Governor and other persons, as 
required or requested by the Governor and approved by the Superintendent. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
The Department shall provide security for the Governor, the Governor’s spouse, the first family, and 
other person(s) designated by the Governor. Department personnel shall attempt to provide any 
assistance requested by the Executive Protection Section (E.P.S.) Commander and/or other E.P.S. 
personnel as approved by the Superintendent. 
 
III. PROCEDURES 
 
A. Investigations of any threat or incident of violence against a person(s) being protected by the E.P.S. 
shall follow procedures outlined in INV-009. 
 
B. The E.P.S. is responsible for, but not limited to, providing the following services for the Governor, 
the Governor’s spouse, the first family, their property, or any person, as requested by the Governor: 
 
1. Providing personal protection and security; 
2. Scheduling, arranging, and/or providing transportation; 
3. Providing security for: 
 
a. Travel to/from and at all sites the Governor and/or the Governor’s spouse are visiting, and 
b. Any security related detail, as requested by the Governor or reasonable request by the Governor’s 
spouse. 
 
4. Coordinating with other police agencies for in-state and out-of-state visits; and 
5. Initiating service and/or repair on the Governor’s/spouse’s official vehicle, as needed. 
 
C. The Superintendent shall assign an E.P.S. Commander. 
 
D. The E.P.S. Commander shall be responsible for, but not limited to: 
 
1. Obtaining and providing transportation and security; 
2. Planning and reconnaissance of primary and alternative travel routes; 
3. Utilizing a schedule that provides maximum protection for protected persons. 
4. Obtaining required personnel to provide adequate security during the transportation to and from 
each site and facility as required or requested; 
5. Coordinating advance security inspections of sites and facilities to be visited;

6. Having knowledge of EMS availability and location of medical facilities applicable to a site, 
facility, or route of travel; 
7. Gathering intelligence information from all affected districts; 
8. Contacting and providing required information to affected local, state, and federal agency officials; 
9. Obtaining and providing designated identification for the personnel assigned to any governor or 
VIP protection detail. 
 
E. All personnel assigned to a protection detail shall: 
 
1. Be at their assigned locations as requested by the E.P.S. detail commander; 
2. Maintain radio and/or telephone contact with the applicable regional dispatch center, command 
center, and other assigned personnel; and 
3. Shall refrain from disseminating confidential information or information not disclosable to 
the public, relating to official duties and responsibilities, without authorization. 
 
F. Any personnel seeking assignment to E.P.S. shall: 
 
a. Be willing to travel the state/country/abroad, as needed; and 
b. Be away from home for extended periods of time, as needed. 
 
G. Special training requests for personnel assigned to the E.P.S. shall be submitted, through channels, 
to the Training Division for appropriate processing; 
 
H. During an extended absence of any member of the E.P.S. (e.g., vacation, training schools, illness) 
another ISP officer shall be assigned by TDY to the E.P.S.  by the Superintendent. 
 
I. CAPITOL POLICE SECTION (CPS) 
 
1. CPS, while performing normally assigned duties shall assist E.P.S. by: 
 
a. Providing security to the State Capitol Building (including the Governor’s office) and the 
Government Complex Campus to include responding to various alarms at these locations; 
 
b. Screening all available US Mail and other packages (with the cooperation of the State contracted 
mail service) for anything suspicious; and 
 
c. Providing security for the Governor, the Governor’s spouse, or other persons (as directed) while on 
the grounds of the Governor’s residence, after hours, or when coordinated between EPS and CPS. 
 
J. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, policies, 
and procedures.
