I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish the mission statement and organizational structure of the Capitol Police Section. 
 
II. POLICY 
  
To establish a mission statement for the Capitol Police Section and their organizational structure. 
 
III. MISSION STATEMENT and HISTORY 
A. The Mission of the Indiana State Police  Capitol Police Section is to  provide optimal 
enforcement services to citizens of Indiana, state employees, those visiting Indiana Government 
Center complex, and other state managed properties in Indianapolis cons istent with the highest 
traditions of the Department. Through our proactive enforcement, investigative, crime prevention 
efforts, we will improve the quality of all life for all Hoosiers and those visiting the Indiana 
Government Center complex and state managed properties in Indianapolis.  
B. The Indiana State Police  Capitol Police Section will be the model of an innovative, patron -
oriented police department.  Our core responsibilities are to prevent crime, maintain order, serve 
victims, and safeguard the I ndiana Government Center complex and state managed properties.  
We will strive to be the best trained and most prepared law enforcement agency in the nation.  
C. Indiana Capitol Police m erged with the Indiana State Police in July 2002 by state legislation 
to b etter provide for the needs of the state in these changing times, today the Capitol Police 
Officer is tasked with providing protection for state elected officials, visiting VIPs, 
and thousands of state employees and visitors daily.   They are charged wi th the enforcement of 
State laws, regulations, and policies.    Capitol Police officers are empowered to exercise full 
police authority in the enforcement of traffic and criminal laws within the state. Primary areas of 
patrol responsibility include the Indiana Government Center Campus; Statehouse; Indiana War 
Memorial and Parks (Veteran's Memorial Plaza, University Park, American Legion Mall, and the 
Soldier and Sailor Monument on the Circle); White River State Park (Indiana State Museum, 
Eiteljorg Museum, N CAA, Military Park, and the Indianapolis Zoo); Indiana State Lab and the 
Indiana State Department of Health.

IV. ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE 
A. The command structure of ranks and grades of Capitol Police Section (CPS) Officers, in 
descending order are:  
 
1. ISP Superintendent  
2. ISP Colonel 
3. ISP Lt. Colonel  
4. ISP Major 
5. ISP Captain 
6. CPS Lieutenant 
7. CPS First Sergeant 
8. CPS Sergeant 
9. CPS Corporal 
10. CPS Officer 
11. CPS Probationary Officer

Officers.
