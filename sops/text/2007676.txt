CAPITOL POLICE SECTION 
OFFICER RULE 3 
 
ASSIGNMENT OF CAPITOL POLICE SECTION OFFICERS 
 
Section 3-1 Best Interest of Department 
 
All vacancies are filled to meet the needs of and the best interest of the Department. 
Section 3-2 Assignment Policy 
Any Capitol Police Section Officer may be assigned or reassigned by the Superintendent 
according to the needs of the Department.
