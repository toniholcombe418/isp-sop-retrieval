ISP Comment Moderation Policy 
Welcome to the an official social media page of the Indiana State Police. This page is maintained 
to provide timely information on safety issues, community events, department initiatives, and 
public notices. It serves as a limited public forum for constructive interaction between the 
Department and members of the public.  
This page is monitored and managed by personnel of the Indiana State Police Department.  
Terms of Use 
By accessing or posting on this page, you agree to the following terms:  
• In case of emergency, dial 911.  
• Do not report crimes via this page. To report a crime or request routine police services, 
please call your local police department or ISP District.  
• Posting information about criminal activity may subject you to subpoena or place you or 
others at risk. Please report crimes through appropriate channels.  
• "Friending" or "liking" this page does not imply endorsement of any person’s views or 
actions by the Department.  
• Comments posted by users do not necessarily reflect the opinions or policies of the 
Department.  
• External links shared on this page do not constitute endorsement by the Department.  
Comment Policy and Moderation 
The Indiana State Police encourages public comments, questions, and concerns. However, all 
discussions must remain civil, respectful, and relevant to the post or Department activities. To 
maintain a safe, welcoming environment, the Department reserves the right to remove comments 
or content that violate viewpoint-neutral rules, including:  
• Incitement to violence or criminal conduct;  
• Threats, harassment, or hate speech;  
• Obscenity or sexually explicit content;  
• Commercial advertisements, solicitations, or spam;  
• Repetitive, disruptive posts ("flooding" or "trolling");  
• Disclosure of confidential, personal, or sensitive information;  
• Content violating copyright or intellectual property rights;  
• Links to malware, harmful software, or phishing sites.
