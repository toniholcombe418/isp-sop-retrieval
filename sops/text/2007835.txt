STANDARD   OPERATING   PROCEDURE 
State   Form   39870( R/ S- 06) 
Reference Number 
INV-007 
Subject 
Call Out Procedure for District Detectives 
Special Instructions 
Replaces INV-018 dated March 1, 2015 
Effective Date 
October 24, 2025 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for the availability of district detectives during non-duty hours. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
The Department shall have personnel who are assigned as district detectives available for call out during non-
duty hours. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Each District Investigative Commander (DIC) shall be responsible to develop a procedure for that 
respective district for the call out of a detective during non-duty hours, e.g., after normal working hours, 
weekends, and holidays. 
 
B. Once established, the procedure shall be inserted in the district’s policy manual and maintained by the 
district duty officer. All district personnel shall be made aware of and follow the policy. 
 
C. The district procedure shall be used when the DIC determines an investigation is of such a nature that a 
detective needs to respond to the crime scene to assume control of or coordinate the investigation after normal 
duty hours for detectives. 
 
D. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, policies, and 
procedures.
