STANDARD   OPERATING   PROCEDURE 
State Form 39870( R/ S- 06) 
Reference Number 
ENF-013 
Subject 
Road, Signal, Sign Defects, and Utility Outages 
Special Instructions 
Replaces ENF-013 dated March 1, 2015 
Effective Date 
   September 15, 2025 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Provide guidelines in identifying potential traffic engineering problems, road, signal, and sign 
defects, and for the reporting of such issues to the proper authorities or public utility. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Enforcement personnel should report all utility outages and road, signal, and sign defects when  
those defects could create a hazardous condition or make travel on the roadway unsafe. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Traffic safety/engineering issues. 
 
1. Enforcement personnel, after learning of a roadway defect or hazard should, at the earliest 
possible time, report the issue to the applicable Regional Dispatch Center (RDC). 
 
2. The RDC shall make an entry into the CAD System. 
 
3. The RDC shall notify the proper agency about the issue and update the CAD entry with  the name 
of the company notified, name of the company’s representative, date, time, and information 
reported about the problem. 
 
4. The CAD entry shall remain active until the issue is repaired or for 24 hours. After 24 hours, if 
there have been no status updates, the RDC shall assign an officer to recheck the issue. If the issue 
has not been repaired, the CAD shall be updated and the information forwarded to the district 
commander. 
 
B. Upon receipt of a non-resolved issue, the district commander shall formally notify  the proper 
agency of the issue.  A copy of this documentation shall be maintained by the reporting officer. 
 
C. Statistical data, available through the Criminal Justice Data Division (CJDD) should be used to 
assist in identifying problem areas that can be addressed, through enforcement, or by providing 
information to the proper traffic-engineering professionals. 
 
D. The district commander shall maintain liaison with highway department personnel to promote 
traffic safety in general and to provide them with statistical information as may be required. 
 
E. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies, and procedures.
