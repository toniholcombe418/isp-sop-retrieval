CAPITOL POLICE SECTION 
OFFICER RULE 8 
HOURS OF WORK 
 
 
Section 8-1 Length of Daily Shifts 
 
Capitol Police Section (CPS) officers shall normally work a daily eight hour shift; unless, 
working the Alternative Work Schedule or other approved schedule. A one-half hour meal break 
shall be included. 
 
Section 8-2 Shift Schedules 
 
A. All shifts shall be scheduled to best satisfy Department needs subject to the approval of the 
Superintendent. 
 
B. Officers shall accept emergency calls and special detail assignments other than regular duty 
scheduled hours. This time shall be designated as overtime worked and shall be eligible for 
proper compensation. 
 
Section 8-3 Work Week 
 
Normal weekly work schedules shall be a minimum of 40 hours, including meal break; unless, 
working the Alternative Work Schedule or other approved schedule. 
 
Section 8-4 Shift Selection (24 hour rotating schedule) 
 
Shift assignments shall be determined by the Capitol Police Section Commander.
