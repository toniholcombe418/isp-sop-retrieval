STANDARD   OPERATING   PROCEDURE 
State   Form   39870( R/ S- 06) 
Reference Number 
INV-020 
Subject 
Special Deputization – Federal Entitlement 
Special Instructions 
Replaces INV-007 dated December 29, 2008 
Effective Date 
March 1, 2015 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for designation of Department employees as entitled to certain federal status. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Department employees may accept designation of federal status to further the investigative mission of 
the Department and to promote cooperative investigations. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. In order to facilitate the assignment of Department personnel to be entitled to the privileges and 
responsibilities incumbent with being designated as persons given access to evidence of a particular 
federal grand jury on a particular investigation in accordance with Rule 6 (e) of the Federal Rules of 
Criminal Procedure; and/or deputization as a special deputy U.S. marshal; the following guidelines 
have been established: 
 
1. A letter requesting permission to entitle any Department personnel to those privileges and 
responsibilities under Rule 6 (e) and/or deputization as a special deputy U.S. marshal shall be 
addressed to the Superintendent of the Indiana State Police Department by the appropriate U.S. 
attorney. 
 
2. The Superintendent and the U.S. attorney shall determine those other persons who may also need to 
be listed, e.g., clerical personnel and/or supervisory personnel. 
 
3. When permission has been granted by the Superintendent for those appropriate personnel, the 
U.S. attorney shall prepare the formal documents needed and complete any other necessary steps. A 
copy of the completed documents shall be forwarded to the Superintendent. 
 
B. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies, and procedures.
