I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines and procedures for the utilization, selection, and training of Indiana 
State  Police (ISP) Capitol Police Section (CPS) Officers as members of the CPS Bicycle 
Patrol. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Officers assigned to the CPS Bicycle Patrol shall be selected and shall operate as outlined in this 
procedure. This program is available to eligible CPS Officers only. 
 
III. DEFINITIONS 
 
A. CPS Command – for the purposes of this SOP, shall be the CPS Lieutenant or First Sergeant. 
 
B. Bicycle Coordinator - A CPS Officer who shall oversee the CPS Bicycle Patrol. 
 
C. Assistant Bicycle Coordinator – A CPS Officer who shall assist with the CPS Bicycle  Patrol. 
 
D. Bicycle Patrol Officer – A CPS Officer assigned and trained for bicycle patrol to perform first 
line police services. 
 
IV. WORK DUTIES 
 
A. Only CPS Officers assigned to and trained for this program are authorized to utilize the 
bicycles or their assigned bike equipment without permission of the CPS Command; 
 
B. Officers shall utilize the Bicycle Patrol to perform first line police services; 
 
C. Bicycle Patrol Officers shall ride when the weather permits and the temperature is above 40 
degrees Fahrenheit and when assigned by the CPS Command, Sergeant, or as requested for 
Special Events; 
 
D. Be expected to participate in Special Event(s) which could include  multiple dates; 
 
E. Must successfully complete an annual refresher course as administered by the Bicycle 
Coordinator. 
 
F. Bicycle Patrol Officers are prohibited (while utilizing the bicycle) from: 
 
1. Being the only officer investigating a vehicle crash on a main thoroughfare without additional 
lighting from a marked commission; 

2  
 
2. Initiating vehicle stops; or 
 
3. Transporting prisoners or other persons. 
 
G. When a Bicycle Patrol Officer makes an arrest which requires incarceration they shall: 
 
1. Have all prisoners transported by a patrol commission or prisoner transport vehicle; 
 
2. Complete all related criminal incident and arrest reports in accordance with INV-003. 
 
H. A Bicycle Patrol Officer shall be temporarily suspended from the CPS Bicycle Patrol when 
recommended by a commander or supervisor due to unsatisfactory job performance by the 
employee. All disciplinary action against a Bicycle Patrol Officer shall be reviewed by the CPS 
Command, employee’s Squad Sergeant, and CPS Bicycle Coordinator to determine continued 
eligibility for the CPS Bicycle Patrol. 
 
V. SELECTION PROCESS 
 
A. When an opening on the CPS Bicycle Patrol exists, the CPS Bicycle Patrol Coordinator shall 
cause notification of all applicable personnel of the vacancy via e-mail a minimum of ten (10) 
days prior to the closing date. Interested personnel shall submit a memo requesting 
consideration with appropriate endorsements and recommendations, through channels, to the 
CPS Bicycle Patrol Coordinator. 
 
1. All applicants must: 
 
a. Be in sufficient physical shape to ride a Department bicycle for extended periods of time; 
 
b. At a minimum, have met all baseline performance standards on their last evaluation; 
 
c. Have successfully graduated from the Indiana Law Enforcement Academy (ILEA) with a 
minimum of six (6) months completed in their probationary status; 
 
d. Be available to work special details as assigned by a member of CPS Command; and 
 
e. Be able to successfully complete the CPS Bicycle Patrol physical abilities/training course test 
as outlined by the International Police Mountain Bike Association (IPMBA). All training and 
testing shall be administered by the CPS Bicycle Patrol Coordinator or a certified IPMBA 
Instructor. 
 
B. The screening procedure shall consist of the IPMBA four-day course.

VI. TRAINING AND MAINTENANCE 
 
A. CPS Bicycle Patrol Officers shall be properly trained and utilized in accordance with the 
standards set forth by the IPMBA. 
 
B. All CPS bicycles shall be maintained by the CPS Bicycle Patrol Coordinator. General 
maintenance (i.e. tire changes, battery replacement and general debris cleaning from chain and 
frame) shall be completed by the riding officer. 
 
VII. UNIFORMS 
 
A. While assigned to bike patrol, all CPS Bicycle Patrol Officers are required to wear 
Department issued and approved bicycle equipment, to include: 
 
1. Protective eyewear; 
2. Helmet; 
3. Nylon duty belt with equipment; 
4. Issued Uniform; 
5. Department issued bike shoe; and 
6. Department issued gloves. 
 
B. All CPS Bicycle Patrol Officers not assigned to the bike patrol shall wear their regular 
Class A uniform. 
 
C. All CPS Bicycle Patrol Officers shall adhere to the Department’s Tattoo Policy as outlined in  
Regulation 6 - Official Responsibilities, Personal Conduct and Relationships of Employees while 
operating a CPS bicycle. 
 
VIII. CHECK SHEETS 
 
A. Officers utilizing CPS bicycles shall complete an electronic “check sheet” before the end of 
their shift and submit it to the CPS Bicycle Coordinator. 
 
B. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies, and procedures.
