I. PURPOSE 
 
To establish duty assignments and responsibilities assigned those positions. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
The Capitol Police Section (“CPS”) shall provide law enforcement services, including security, 
to Indiana Government Center properties and other state-owned properties in downtown 
Indianapolis.  CPS shall respond to calls at these locations, assist visitors, and take proactive 
measures to ensure the safety of all staff and visitors. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. CPS shall provide services at the following locations: 
 
1. Indiana State Capitol building; 
2. Indiana Government Center North and South, including parking facilities; 
3. IDOA warehouse and state motor pool; 
4. Indiana State Library; 
5. Indiana State Archives building; 
6. Indiana Historical Society; 
7. Indiana State Police Laboratory; 
8. Department of Workforce Development; 
9. State Department of Health; 
10. The Governor’s residence; 
11. State offices in the PNC building, to include: IURC, IN Tax Court, IN Court of Appeals, and 
Office of Utility Counselor; 
12. State memorials, to include: the IN War Memorial, Soldiers and Sailors Monument, 
University Park, Military Park, 9/11 Memorial, USS Indianapolis Memorial, Medal of Honor 
Memorial, Veteran’s Plaza, and the American Legion Mall; 
13. Properties associated with White River State Park, to include: the Indianapolis Zoo, White 
River State Park, the State Museum, the Eiteljorg Museum, NCAA Hall of Champions, and 
Victory Field; 
14. Other buildings or properties used by the State as directed by the Governor or Superintendent 
of the Indiana State Police; and 
15. Locations as assigned by special details, such as the Indy 500. 
 
B. Officers assigned to an Entry Control Points (“ECP”) shall be responsible for the following: 
 
1. Monitor access cards use, visitor entrance (if applicable), and foot traffic in the general area;

2. Watch for prohibited items and suspicious packages; 
 
3. Take enforcement action when necessary, such as stopping an individual with a weapon; 
 
4. Remain observant and alert to their surroundings; 
 
5. Conduct themselves professionally by: 
 
a. Answering visitors’ or employees’ inquiries; 
b. Provide assistance when requested or necessary by assigned private security personnel at the 
public ECPs on the government campus. 
 
6. Ensure the ECP operates functionally and address and disruptions with visitors that become 
disruptive to operations of the ECP. 
 
7. Close the East ECP, if there is an emergency situation within the Governor’s Office.  The 
officer shall immediately close the point and respond to the Governor’s Office and advise 
dispatch via radio. 
 
C. Officers assigned to the Governor’s Residence shall be responsible for the following: 
 
1. Ensuring the safety and security of the Governor,* First Family, the residence, staff and 
visitors; 
 
2. Monitoring the alarms and cameras in the guard house; 
 
3. Perform periodic patrols of the grounds and residence; 
 
4. Call for another officer to take enforcement action, if necessary. 
 
*NOTE – The primary responsibility shall always be the security of the Governor and First 
Family. 
 
D. Officers assigned to patrol shall be responsible for the following: 
 
1. Complete patrol on foot, bike, or vehicle as assigned;  
 
2. Checking the interior and exterior of the properties for unsecured doors, and suspicious or 
criminal activity; 
 
3. During business hours, offer relief to officers stationed at the ECPs before leaving the 
building; 
 
4. During non-business hours, checking the interior for problems such as leaks and reporting the 
issue to dispatch; 
 
5. Keep dispatch advised of their location through mobile CAD while patrolling; 
 
6.  Provide information and directions to visitors as requested; 
 
7. Refrain from any activity that would draw them away from their primary responsibility of 
security of state property and safety of the employees and visitors; 
 
8.  Enforce the no smoking policy by advising the location is a no smoking area; 
 
9. Respond to Calls for Services on the properties listed under III. A.  
 
E. CPS Officer in Charge (OIC) shall be responsible for the following: 
 
1. Remain on the Indiana Government Center campus until relieved by the next OIC/shift 
sergeant (the OIC may leave the campus to respond to calls for a supervisor or if there not 
enough manpower to respond to a Call for Service); 
 
3. Advise the next OIC/shift sergeant of any ongoing matters that may need to be addressed; 
 
4. Complete the CPS Shift Activity report at the end of each shift; 
 
5. Ensure all units have properly reported for the beginning of their shifts; 
 
6.  Advise the appropriate shift sergeant of any call-offs or no shows by CPS officers; 
 
7. Ensure CPS officers are wearing the CPS daily uniform in compliance with CPS-001 and 
report to the shift sergeant any violation; 
 
8. Whenever CPS K-9 units are unavailable, the first shift OIC shall be responsible for 
completing IGC mailroom checks at 0915 and 1215 hours, Monday through Friday. 
9. Any activity of a serious and/or sensitive nature that might garner media attention shall 
immediately be brought to the attention of the shift sergeant(s), via telephone call and email. 
When appropriate, and a shift sergeant cannot be reached, chain of command is to be utilized 
beginning with the Assistant Section Commander; 
 
10. The OIC may grant use of sick leave for requesting officers when there is sufficient 
manpower for shift coverage (all other leave must be granted by the shift sergeant or member of 
the command staff). 
 
F. An officer assigned to CPS Investigations shall be responsible for the following: 
 
1. Completing duties regularly assigned to an ISP Detective, but specific to crimes occurring on 
the properties identified under III. A., such as: interviews, evidence collections, surveillance, 
completing investigative reports, identification of suspects, assisting in prosecution of suspects, 
and service of search warrants. 
 
2. Assisting other CPS officers in furtherance of their investigations; 
 
3. Report to the Indianapolis Field Investigations Investigative Squad Leader. 
 
G. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies and procedures.
