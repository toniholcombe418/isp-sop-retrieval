I. PURPOSE 
 
To establish the Field Training Program for CPS Officers. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
New hires to CPS shall complete a Field Training Program as outlined below.  Officers shall be 
evaluated by their Primary Field Training Officer (FTO) and the Field Training Operations 
Coordinator (FTOC).  Probationary Officers (POs) who fail to or who are unable to complete all 
requirements of the Field Training Program, or remedial training, shall be terminated from 
employment with the Department. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Selection: 
 
1. Field Training Officers (Selection of) FTO candidates shall: 
 
a. Be chosen by the CPS Commander based on skills, knowledge, abilities, and total job 
performance; 
b. Have completed one year of Capitol Police duties (an exception may be made for an FTO 
Candidate upon recommendation by the CPS Captain); 
c. Have a Baseline Performance or higher rating in all categories on their most recent 
performance evaluation; 
d. Have good writing skills; 
e. Be able to accurately assess the performance of POs; and 
f. Complete an FTO school sponsored, approved, and/or conducted by the Training Division. 
 
2. Field Training Officer Coordinators (Selection of) FTOCs shall: 
 
a. Hold the rank of sergeant or above; 
b. Be selected by the CPS Commander; 
c. Have received a Baseline Performance or higher rating in all categories on their most recent 
performance evaluation; 
d. Have good writing skills; 
e. Be able to accurately assess the performance of FTOs and POs; and 
f. Complete training regarding the management of the Field Training Program. 
 
B. All new officers to the Indiana State Capitol Police Section must complete and pass a Field 
Training Program prior to conducting solo patrol.

C. The standard length of the FTO program will be determined by the FTOC and CPS 
Commander, but will generally be five (5) weeks. 
 
D. The CPS Task Book shall be the guide all Field Training Officers (FTOs) use to instruct their 
Probationary Officers. 
 
E. Probationary Officer(s) shall be trained in accordance with the CPS Task Book. 
 
F. The first three (3) days of FTO are non-scoring and will not correspond to a Daily Observation 
Report (DOR); otherwise, each observed day of FTO shall be documented in PowerFTOs by the 
assigned Field Training Officer and in reference to the provided Standard Evaluation Guide 
(SEG) scoring. 
 
G. To ensure satisfactory completion of the Task Book, the FTOC, Primary FTO, or other 
supervisors shall conduct regular checks to ensure each PO’s definite progress. 
 
H. At the end of the FTO period, the primary Field Training Officer shall complete a 
recommendation for the release or extension of their PO’s training and deliver it to the FTOC for 
review. 
 
I. POs shall complete their own professional reviews of each FTO and deliver it to the FTOC. 
The standard form for the FTO review shall be set by the FTOC and provided to the PO prior to 
the final week of FTO. 
 
J. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, 
rules, policies and procedures.
