STANDARD   OPERATING   PROCEDURE 
State   Form   39870( R/ S- 06) 
Reference Number 
INV-024 
Subject 
Violent Gang and Terrorist Organization File (VGTOF) 
Special Instructions 
Replaces INV-024 dated April 4, 2024 
Effective Date 
October 24, 2025 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for the utilization of identifying information pertaining to violent criminal gangs 
and terrorist organizations and their particular members and controls for the handling of information 
identifying potential danger posed by violent individuals and organizations to law enforcement 
officers. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
The Violent Gang and Terrorist Organization File (VGTOF) shall be maintained by the ISP 
Intelligence Section (IS) and may be used by enforcement personnel for authorized purposes. 
Information obtained will serve to warn law enforcement officers of the potential danger posed by 
violent individuals and their specific organizations.  
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. Law enforcement personnel shall report VGTOF information to the ISP IS by email to an intel 
analyst and/or the Indiana Intelligence Fusion Center (IIFC). 
 
B. ISP IS personnel shall follow the criteria established by National Crime Information Center 
(NCIC). The criteria identified to support an entry should be documented for the purposes of 
validation and audit. Additionally, each gang member criteria (GMC) record must include a notation 
in the Criteria for Entry (ECR) field. The following criteria must exist with respect to any individual, 
whether a gang or terrorist organization member, to be entered in the GMC: 
 
1. A gang name/group (GNG) or subgroup (SGP) must match a previously entered gang or terrorist 
organization in the GRC. 
 
a. The Group Reference Capability (GRC) contains identification and information about gangs or 
terrorist organizations. Three (3) criteria must be met by a group before information may be entered in 
the GRC: 
 
(i) Must be an ongoing organization, association, or group of three or more persons; 
(ii) The group must be engaged in or show a pattern of conduct which involves the use of force; and 
(iii) The purpose of the group, in using violence, must be to intimidate or coerce a government, 
civilian population or segment thereof in the furtherance of political or social objectives.  
 
b. Group Member Capability (GMC). An individual must meet the following criteria to be considered 
a candidate for entry into the NCIC-VGTOF: 
 
(i) Membership in a criminal gang, terrorist organization or a subgroup thereof and is entered in the 
GRC; and 
(ii) Has admitted membership in that gang or terrorist organization (or subgroup) at the time of the 
member’s arrest or incarceration; or meets two (2) of the following: 
 

 
a) Has been identified by an individual of proven reliability as a group member; 
b) Has been identified by an individual of unknown reliability as a group member and that information 
has been corroborated in significant respects; 
c) Has been observed by members of the entering agency to frequent a known group’s area, associate 
with known group members and/or affect that group’s style of dress, tattoos, hand signals, or symbols; 
d) Has been arrested on more than one occasion with known group members for offenses consistent 
with group activity; and/or 
e) Has admitted membership in the identified group at any time other than arrest or incarceration. 
 
C. All documentation shall be maintained by Department personnel in accordance with current 
NCIC/IDACS procedures on maintaining records for the audit. 
 
1. All GMC records are generally subject to a purge date that is five (5) years from the date of entry. 
For exceptions refer to the IDACS Manual. 
 
D. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies, and procedures.
