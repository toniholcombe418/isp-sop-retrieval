I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines for the utilization, selection, and training of ISP police employees as 
members of the Department’s Motorcycle Patrol (MP). 
 
II. POLICY 
 
MP personnel shall provide the Department with the capability of concentrated traffic 
enforcement, and quick initial response to emergency calls for service. The unique capabilities 
and the professional image of the motorcycle patrol will be utilized, as needed, for the benefit of 
the Department and the general public. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. MP personnel shall be assigned to the Collaborative Policing Section.  The Collaborative 
Policing Commander shall make operational assignments based upon the needs of the 
Department and individual Districts. 
 
B. Duties of motorcycle patrol personnel shall include but are not limited to: 
 
1. Traffic enforcement in areas with high volumes of traffic violations or traffic crashes; 
2. Initial response to emergency calls for service; 
3. Providing traffic and criminal enforcement at special details including but not limited to:   
 
a. Indianapolis 500 
b. Indiana State Fair,  
c. Brickyard 400, etc;  
 
4. Providing escorts and security details as needed or directed. 
 
C. Requests for MP personnel at special details or public appearances shall be coordinated 
through the Collaborative Policing Commander. 
 
D. The Collaborative Policing Commander shall determine the number of MC team members 
and their geographical breakdown. All MP assignment information shall be published in the MP 
Operational/Training Manual. 
 
E. Selection Process for Vacancies 
 
1. When an MP vacancy exists, the Collaborative Policing Commander shall notify the division, 

2  
section, zone, area, and district commanders by email.  
 
2. Those commanders shall advertise the vacancy a minimum of five (5) days prior to the closing 
date.  
 
3. Interested personnel shall submit a Specialty Team Application, with appropriate 
endorsements and recommendations, through channels, to the Collaborative Policing 
Commander. 
 
4. All applicants must: 
 
a. Meet the minimum standards as defined in SOC-004; 
b. Be willing to sign a contract committing to the MP Program for a minimum of two (2) years; 
c. Have had no preventable Department crashes within the past 24 months; 
d. Be able to provide a secure inside facility to store the assigned motorcycle; 
e. Attach copies of the following items to the Specialty Team Application when submitted: 
 
(1) A copy of a valid driver’s license, showing a current motorcycle endorsement. 
(2) The date the applicant’s original motorcycle endorsement was received. 
(3)A detailed description of applicant’s riding experience, and types of motorcycles operated. 
 
f. Be in good physical condition and able to successfully complete the physical abilities test.  
 
5. The Collaborative Policing Commander shall notify the candidates and their commanders, at 
least seven (7) days prior to the date of the selection procedure and furnish information listing 
the time, date, and location of the interview and test. 
 
a. The selection procedure will consist of an interview and physical abilities test. The interview 
committee shall be selected by the Collaborative Policing Commander and shall consist of a 
three (3) person committee consisting of a committee chair and two (2) committee members. The 
physical abilities test shall be administered by the MP team leaders and the Training Section’s 
Health Management Specialist, using a Department authorized motorcycle. 
 
(1). After having been shown the proper method, the applicant shall: 
 
(a) Properly mount the motorcycle from each side; 
(b) Properly upright a downed motorcycle and set the side stand from each side; and 
(c) Physically push the motorcycle forward 100 feet, and backward 25 feet. 
 
g. After the interviews and testing, the Collaborative Policing Commander shall make the final 
selection and shall notify the person chosen for the position, in writing, through channels. 
 
h. Upon selection, new MP team members shall be assigned to the appropriate MP team.
