STANDARD   OPERATING   PROCEDURE 
State   Form   39870( R/ S- 06) 
Reference Number 
LEG-001 
Subject 
Department Name and/or Symbols – Use of 
Special Instructions 
Replaces LEG-001 dated March 10, 2008 
Effective Date 
April 4, 2024 
 
I. PURPOSE 
 
Establish guidelines to limit and protect the use of the Department name and symbol. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
Neither the Department nor any employee shall knowingly associate the Indiana State Police name 
or symbol with any product or service for commercial purposes without the written approval of the 
Superintendent. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. The Department, with approval of the Superintendent, may allow its name or symbols to be 
associated with the name or symbols of a product or service when the main purpose of such 
association is in the best interest of the Department and such association will not reflect discredit 
on the Department. 
 
B. If the Department does knowingly approve the association of its name or symbols with the 
name or symbols of a product or service, it will be clearly understood that the Department will 
neither enter into, nor be responsible for any financial arrangements made in connection therewith, 
nor will it guarantee the performance of any product or contract made in connection therein. 
 
C. The Superintendent, upon written notice, may withdraw any approval for use of the Department 
name or symbols. 
 
D. Department personnel shall not knowingly use or display the Department name or symbols in 
any manner (personal or commercial), outside the course of day to day work related activities, 
which would bring discredit or embarrassment to the Department. 
 
E. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies, and procedures.
