I. PURPOSE 
 
To establish requirements for the training and probationary period of Tier II CPS Officers. 
 
II. POLICY 
 
CPS may employ Tier II officers in accordance with the Indiana Law Enforcement 
Administrative Board approval effective May 2023.  These officers shall complete the training 
outlined below prior to beginning shift assignments as a CPS Officer.  Tier II CPS Officers shall 
be restricted during their probationary period as outlined below. 
 
III. PROCEDURE 
 
A. A Tier II CPS officer must graduate from the ILEA Tier II 10-week Academy. 
 
B. Upon completion of the Tier II Academy, the CPS officer shall complete the CPS Finishing 
School. 
 
1.  The Finishing School shall consist of 3-5 weeks of specialized training as outlined in the CPS 
Finishing School Training. 
 
C. Tier II CPS Officers shall also complete any State mandated trainings, such as SuccessFactors 
Sexual Harassment, Ethics, Resource Use Agreement, and others. 
 
D. Upon completion of the above required training, CPS Officers shall be assigned a Field 
Training Officer in accordance with CPS-006. 
 
E. Tier II CPS Officers shall be in a probationary status for one year in accordance with HMR-
020. 
 
F. During this probationary period there are restrictions which will be followed by all Tier II 
CPS officers: 
 
1. CPS officers may carry their duty issued weapons in an off-duty status in accordance with ISP 
TRA-001. 
 
2. Overtime shall be permitted to all probationary officers if it is related to their duties and 
responsibilities as a CPS officer on the Indiana Government properties. Probationary CPS 
officers shall follow the ISP overtime HMR-008.

3. During the probationary period, while off-duty a Tier II CPS officer shall not engage in any 
type of enforcement activities unless it is an emergency which requires legal intervention by the 
officer to prevent the loss of life, protect innocent victims or assist another officer during 
enforcement of felonious criminal activity. Upon the conclusion of any actions taken by a 
probationary CPS officer, they shall immediately contact their immediate CPS supervisor and 
inform them of the incident. 
 
4. Tier II CPS officer shall not work any security related non-department employment during 
their probationary period.  Upon conclusion of probation, they may submit a request in 
accordance with HMR-030. 
 
5. Prior to the conclusion of the CPS officer’s probationary period, the CPS chain of command 
shall hold a review board. The purpose of this review is to assess the CPS Officer’s performance 
during the probationary period and assist the CPS chain of command in the recommendation to 
end the CPS Officer’s probationary period.  
 
G. This procedure is to be used in conjunction with all relevant Department regulations, rules, 
policies and procedures.
