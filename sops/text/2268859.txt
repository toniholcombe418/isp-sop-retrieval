REGULATION 4 
 
COMMAND STRUCTURE, ORGANIZATION, AND FUNCTIONS 
 
A. The command functions of the Department shall be vested in the following positions which take 
precedence in the order named and with the control indicated. 
 
B. The Superintendent, as chief administrative officer of the Department, shall perform all of the 
duties of the office as prescribed by law and exercise general control and management over all 
functions of the Department.  The Superintendent shall have command authority over all employees. 
 
B. The Assistant Superintendent shall maintain in effective operation, the approved regulations, 
rules, policies, and procedures of the Department; exercise general supervision over the functions 
assigned by the Superintendent; exercising command authority over all employees or any portion 
thereof, and perform any other duties designated by the Superintendent. 
 
C. The Executive and Personal Staff shall maintain, in effective operation, the approved 
regulations, rules, policies, and procedures of the Department; exercise supervision over the 
applicable Divisions, Areas, Districts, Sections, and functions under their command; and exercise 
command authority over all employees in their respective administrative jurisdictions; and perform 
any other duties designated by the Superintendent or Assistant Superintendent. 
 
D. Deputy Superintendents shall maintain, in effective operation, the approved regulations, rules, 
policies, and procedures of the Department; exercise supervision over the division and sections 
under their command; exercise command authority over all employees in their respective Bureaus; 
and perform other duties designated by the Superintendent or Assistant Superintendent 
 
E. The Division Commanders shall maintain, in effective operation, the approved regulations, 
rules, policies, and procedures of the Department; exercise supervision over the division and 
sections under their command; and perform other duties designated by the Superintendent, Assistant 
Superintendent, or Deputy Superintendent.  They shall have direct and primary command over the 
employees assigned to their division, and secondary supervisory control, through prescribed 
channels, over employees who may not be assigned to their division, but whose functions are 
partially governed by their division. 
 
F. The Deputy Division Commanders and Section Commanders shall maintain, in effective 
operation, the approved regulations, rules, policies, and procedures of the Department; exercise 
supervision over the sections under their command; and perform other duties designated by the 
applicable Superintendent, Assistant Superintendent, Deputy Superintendent, or Division 
Commander. . 
 
G. The Executive and Personal Staff shall directly advise the Superintendent on operational and 
policy matters concerning the administration of the Department. 
 
H. The Area Commanders shall be under the direct supervision of the appropriate Division 
Commander and shall have direct command over the employees assigned to them and the  districts 
within their area.

(2) "District Commanders" shall have direct command over the troopers and civilian employees

(3) "District Investigative Commanders" shall have direct command over the investigative

(4) "District Duty Officers" shall act on routine or emergency situations on behalf of the district

I. When absent from duty, commanders shall appoint an acting commander from the next available 
lower rank. In the event a commander is incapacitated, their commanding officer shall appoint an 
acting commander. 
 
J. When a position of division commander has been vacated, the appropriate deputy superintendent 
shall appoint an acting commander. 
 
K. When the position of a deputy commander or area captain has been vacated, the appropriate 
deputy superintendent shall appoint an acting deputy commander or area captain from the next 
available rank. 
 
L. When the position of a district commander has been vacated, the appropriate area commander 
shall appoint an acting district commander from the next available rank. 
 
M. When the position of an area investigative commander or a district investigative commander is 
vacated, the applicable investigative commander shall appoint an acting commander from the next 
available rank. 
 
N. Acting commanders shall have full authority to act in such capacity until relieved of that 
command.

P. The command structure of ranks and grades of Capitol Police Section (CPS) Officers, in 
descending order are: 
 
(1) ISP Superintendent  
(2)  ISP Colonel 
(3) ISP Lt. Colonel  
(4) ISP Major 
(5)  ISP Captain 
(6)  CPS Lieutenant 
(7) CPS First Sergeant 
(8)  CPS Sergeant 
(9)  CPS Corporal 
(10)  CPS Officer 
(11)  CPS Probationary Officer 
 
Q. Employees shall obey any lawful order of a superior, including any order relayed from a  
superior by an employee of the same or lesser rank/grade. 
 
R. In situations where two or more police employees of the same rank/grade work together, the  
senior police employee shall be in command of the detail, unless otherwise designated. 
 
S. In conducting the routine business of the Department, the command structure reflected on the 
organizational chart shall be followed. 
 
T. Upon receiving conflicting lawful orders, a police employee shall inform the officer in command 
of the current detail of the conflicting orders and shall ask for resolution of the conflict. If seeking 
resolution is impractical, the police employee shall follow the most recent lawful order. 
 
U. To ensure that all appropriate commanders are fully apprised of Department business, matters of 
a routine nature, ascending and descending, shall be routed through and/or cleared through the 
proper chain of command. 
 
V. Employees shall courteously refer to their commanding officer if or when any non-department  
person seeks to give them orders. 
 
W. The organizational structure of the Department shall be a formal framework through which 
organizational components are arranged, defined, directed, and coordinated. The organizational 
structure shall be detailed in the Department's Organizational Chart or CPS Organizational Chart. 
The Organizational chart shall be reviewed and updated annually or when organizational changes 
dictate.
